Favorly ("we") is run by Hantian Zhang for Cornell Tech Team 402. This policy explains what the Favorly app collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

What we collect

  • Your account. Sign in with Apple gives us an identifier for your Apple ID. We don't ask Apple for your name or email.
  • Your profile. The display name you choose, that you confirmed you are 18 or older, and which version of the Terms you accepted.
  • What you post. Your requests (title, details, category, and the neighborhood you choose), the messages you send, the reviews you write, and the reports and blocks you make.
  • Approximate location. When you browse or post, the app reads your location and rounds it to about 100 meters on your phone. Only the rounded point is sent, and only with a request you post or to find requests near you. We don't track your location in the background.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, your device's push token, so we can tell you when someone picks up your request, messages you, completes a favor or reviews you.

We don't use analytics or advertising tools, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your data for advertising.

Who sees what

  • Other users see your display name, Kindness score, reviews about you, your completed favors, and your open requests with their neighborhood and a rounded distance. They never see your exact location or your Apple ID.
  • Messages are visible only to the requester and the helper of that request.
  • Reports are seen only by our moderators.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase stores the data on servers in the United States (US East).
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and delivers notifications.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deleting it (Profile → Delete account) removes your profile, your requests that aren't completed, the messages and reviews you wrote, reviews about you, your blocks and your devices. Favors you completed with someone else stay in their history, with your side shown as from a deleted user. Reports may be kept up to a year to keep the community safe, and longer if the law requires.

Your choices

  • Turn off location or notifications at any time in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Delete your account in the app, or email us to ask for a copy of your data or a correction.

Age

Favorly is for people 18 and older. If you think someone under 18 is using it, tell us and we'll remove the account.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app before it takes effect.
