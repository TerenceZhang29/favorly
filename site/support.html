Something unsafe happened

If anyone is in danger, call 911 first. Then report the request, message or person in the app (the … menu, or long-press a message), or email us. Reports are reviewed every day.

Common questions

  • How do I block someone? Open their profile or one of their requests, tap … and choose Block. Undo it in Profile → Blocked people.
  • Why is my location off? Favorly needs your location to show requests near you. Turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Favorly.
  • Who sees my location? Others see only the neighborhood you choose and a rounded distance. Your position is rounded to about 100 meters before it leaves your phone.
  • Notifications don't arrive. Check Settings → Notifications → Favorly. Favorly asks after your first post or pick-up.
  • How do I delete my account? In the app: Profile → Delete account. It deletes your profile, open requests, and the messages and reviews you wrote.
